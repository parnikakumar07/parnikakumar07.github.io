The last page

The art in this storybook isn't mine.

I'm not a UI/UX designer, and I didn't want to pretend to be one. Every landscape here comes from Sky: Children of Light — my favourite game, and the reason this site exists in this shape. The code, the words and the journey are mine; the light belongs to the people below.
